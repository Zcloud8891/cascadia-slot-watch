          FAQ

          Is this the official Global Entry site?

          No. Cascadia Slot Watch is an independent alert service and is not affiliated with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) or DHS. We only read the public appointment availability. You book on the official site, ttp.cbp.dhs.gov, and booking is free.

          What do I get for $7?

          30 days of instant push alerts on a private channel only you know, for the centers you pick (Portland, Seattle, Blaine, Boise), with an optional "only before this date" filter. One payment, no subscription.

          What if nothing opens?

          If no opening matches your centers and date filter during your 30 days, the $7 is refunded to your card automatically on day 31. You don't have to ask.

          How fast are the alerts?

          We check each center once a minute. Paid alerts go out on the same check, so usually within a minute or two of the slot appearing. Free alerts are sent about 10 minutes later and at most once an hour per center. Slots can still be taken before you get there; we promise an alert, not an appointment.

          What is ntfy?

          ntfy is a free, open-source notification app for iPhone and Android. You subscribe to a channel name and get pushes, with no account, phone number or email. That is why alerts cost us almost nothing and why the price is $7.
