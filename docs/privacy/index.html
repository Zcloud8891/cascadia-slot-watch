Privacy

What we store

For buyers: the Stripe checkout reference, the centers and date you pick, your private channel name, alert counts and refund status. We don't ask for your name, phone number or CBP login. Stripe handles your card and receipt under its own privacy policy.

Free alerts and the tap counter

When you tap a free subscribe button, the site records the center and a random ID stored on your device, so we can count signups without counting you twice. No name, email or IP address is saved by us.

Notifications

Alerts are delivered by ntfy.sh, a third-party service, under its own privacy policy. Free channels are public by design. Your paid channel name is random and private; keep it to yourself.

Live openings

The live lists are loaded by your browser directly from the official CBP scheduler.

Not affiliated with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) or DHS. You book interviews yourself, for free, on the official site ttp.cbp.dhs.gov.
