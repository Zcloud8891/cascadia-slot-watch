Refund policy

The automatic refund

Instant alerts last 30 days from checkout. If, in that time, no new opening matched the centers and date filter you chose, we refund the full $7 to your original payment method on about day 31. You don't need to email or fill anything in. Banks usually show it in 5 to 10 days.

What counts as an opening

A new interview time that appears on the official scheduler at one of your centers (and on or before your date, if you set one) while your alerts are on. If an opening matched, the 30 days were delivered and there is no automatic refund, even if someone else booked the slot first.

Anything else

Charged twice, or something went wrong? Reply to your Stripe receipt email and we will make it right.

Not affiliated with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) or DHS. You book interviews yourself, for free, on the official site ttp.cbp.dhs.gov.
